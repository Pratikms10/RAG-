Harborline Transit - Depot Calibration Memo

On 22 October, Depot 7 will calibrate its ticket validators between 13:00 and 15:00. During that window, operators should issue paper transfer slips if a validator rejects a valid pass.

The calibration lead is Maya Deshmukh. The work is limited to platforms 2 and 3; platform 1 remains in normal service.

For questions about annual leave or employee payroll, this memo provides no policy or contact information.
