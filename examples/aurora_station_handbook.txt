Aurora Station Field Handbook

The Aurora Station safety drill begins at 09:30 every Tuesday in the east assembly bay. The safety officer checks attendance before the group returns to work.

The battery laboratory uses cobalt-free sodium-ion cells for short duration backup power. Each cabinet must be inspected every 30 days by a trained technician.

Visitors must wear a yellow badge and remain with an escort in the instrument hall. The cafeteria is closed on Sundays.
